# Sensorimotor coordination in psychosis

This companion to the clinical study compares normative healthy dyads with dyads composed of a patient with psychosis and a healthy partner. Sixteen normative and 15 clinical dyads contributed behavioural data. Fifteen dyads per cohort contributed EEG data. In EEG analyses, Leader and Follower response periods describe event timing. The actor providing a signal is specified separately.

The clinical code covers behavioural response times, tonic and phasic EEG, covariance sensitivity, ΦID and clinical associations. The four main ΦID contrasts and the response-period × condition interaction have separate target inventories and correction families. The [workflow guide](WORKFLOWS.md) describes target selection, surrogate controls, sensitivity analyses and the preparation of Supplementary Tables ST12–ST16.

The [normative reference directory](normative-references/) contains group-level target inventories, control and sensitivity source code, public methods specifications, the ordered map inventory and permutation signs. These materials support inspection of the cohort comparison. Computing study estimates requires authorized participant-level inputs or saved upstream results, depending on the workflow.

Supplementary Tables ST19a–b report selected results from the [complete main clinical associations](results/Complete_main_associations_309.csv). Supplementary Table ST20 reports selected results from the [separate response-period × condition interaction](results/Complete_genuine_interaction_associations_35.csv).

Start with the [reproduction instructions](REPRODUCE.md) to identify the inputs your analysis requires. The [input schema](INPUTS.md) and [environment notes](ENVIRONMENT.md) describe file layouts and software dependencies. Participant-level inputs may be requested from the corresponding author, subject to consent, ethics and institutional data-sharing requirements.
